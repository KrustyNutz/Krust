# Income Strategies — Burnout-Friendly, 30-Day Runway, Online-Only

You have a computer, internet, ~30 days, and you're burned out. This plan is built
around that — not around hustle culture. The rule: **pick paths you can sustain
showing up for**, not the highest theoretical $/hr.

---

## Week 1 — Stop the bleeding, line up income

Do these *before* you apply to anything. They buy you breathing room and they're
free money on the table.

- **Call every creditor / utility / landlord.** Ask for a hardship plan, payment
  deferral, or reduced payment. Almost every big company has one. Script:
  *"Hi, I'm going through a financial hardship. What hardship or deferral
  programs do you have available?"* Card issuers, mortgages, auto loans, power,
  internet, phone, medical bills — all of them.
- **Check what you qualify for.** Run yourself through:
  - [benefits.gov](https://www.benefits.gov/) — federal programs screener
  - [findhelp.org](https://www.findhelp.org/) — local food, rent, utility help by ZIP
  - SNAP (food), Medicaid, LIHEAP (heating/cooling), state unemployment
- **Sell what you don't use.** Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Mercari, Decluttr
  (books/tech), BackMarket (phones/laptops). 1–2 days of effort can be
  $200–$2000.
- **Pause autopay subscriptions.** Rocket Money or just a manual sweep. Free
  money every month.

This isn't "income strategy" but it's the highest $/effort thing you can do
this week, and it doesn't require energy you don't have.

## Tier 1 — Apply this week (best fit for burnout + computer)

These are async, low/no-customer-contact, real money, and hiring right now.

### AI data labeling / RLHF work — highest priority

Companies training AI models pay for humans to rate, write, and critique
outputs. Async, work whenever, no meetings, no customers.

- **DataAnnotation.tech** — apply, pass a writing/reasoning assessment, get
  paid $20–40+/hr for async tasks
- **Outlier.ai** (Scale AI) — similar; specializations pay more
- **Mercor** — matches you to AI training projects
- **Handshake / Surge AI / Invisible Technologies** — same space

Apply to all of them in one sitting. Acceptance is the bottleneck; once you're
in one or two, you can fill hours on your own schedule.

### Online tutoring / teaching (set your own hours)

- **Outschool** — teach kids classes you design yourself. Highest autonomy.
- **Wyzant** — 1:1 tutoring, you set the rate ($30–80/hr typical)
- **Preply** — language/subject tutoring
- **Cambly / Cambly Kids** — casual English conversation, no lesson plan, lowest
  effort entry point ($10–12/hr but truly minimal prep)

### Async / chat-only customer support

Email and chat is way less draining than phone for most burned-out people.

- **Boldly**, **Working Solutions**, **ModSquad** — remote support
- **Liveops** — some chat-only queues
- Search "remote chat support" on **WeWorkRemotely**, **RemoteOK**, **Himalayas**

### User testing (the calmest money on this list)

Record yourself using a website for 15 min, get paid $10–60 per test. Truly on
your schedule. Nobody talks back.

- **UserTesting.com**, **UserInterviews.com**, **Respondent.io**, **Userlytics**,
  **PlaytestCloud** (games), **Prolific** (research studies)

Sign up for *all* of them — invites are sporadic on any single platform, but
together they add up.

## Tier 2 — Build alongside Tier 1

Slower ramp (4–8 weeks to real money) but more sustainable and higher ceiling.

- **Freelance writing / editing** — Contently, nDash, Upwork (niche down hard;
  generalists drown). $50–200+ per piece once established.
- **Transcription / captioning** — Rev, GoTranscript, Scribie. Low pay
  ($0.30–1/audio min) but fully async and easy to start same-day.
- **Virtual assistant** — Time etc, Belay, Fancy Hands. Async task-based VA
  work avoids the calendar-juggling stress of executive VA roles.
- **Bookkeeping** — if you have any accounting comfort, Bookminders / Belay /
  local small businesses on Upwork. Quiet, focused, well-paid.
- **Notion templates / digital products** — Gumroad, Etsy. Slow but compounds
  into passive income.

## Tier 3 — If you have specific skills, lean hard here

Higher pay, but only worth pursuing if it's already in you:

- **Code / dev** → Upwork, Toptal (if senior), Contra, Codementor
- **Design** → 99designs, Dribbble, Contra
- **Spreadsheets / Excel** → surprisingly well-paid niche on Upwork
- **Voice work** → Voices.com, Voice123 (home mic is enough to start)
- **Resume writing / LinkedIn rewrites** → high demand, $100–300 per resume

## A realistic 30-day cash projection

If you do Week 1 + apply to ~6 Tier 1 platforms in week 1:

| Day  | Likely state                                                    |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1–3  | Hardship deferrals approved → bills off your back for 30–90 days |
| 4–7  | First user-testing payouts hit ($50–200)                        |
| 7–14 | Accepted to 1–2 AI labeling platforms; start logging hours      |
| 14–21| First full week of labeling pay ($300–800 depending on hours)   |
| 21–30| Second pay cycle + any tutoring/chat-support role starts        |

Realistic 30-day total for ~20 hrs/week of effort: **$1,200–$3,000**, plus
whatever you sold and whatever bills you got pushed out.

## Rules for burned-out mode

1. **Two hours of focused work beats eight hours of distracted work.** Don't
   measure the day in hours sat down; measure it in tasks completed.
2. **Don't stack 3 customer-facing gigs.** Pick at most one human-contact role
   and pad the rest with async work.
3. **Apply in batches, not one at a time.** One afternoon = apply to 8–10
   platforms. Then stop applying for a week and let responses come in.
4. **Set a hard stop time every day.** Burnout deepens without one. Even 7pm
   counts.
5. **One "real human" check-in per week minimum** — friend, family, therapist,
   doctor, hotline. Money pressure narrows your thinking; another voice
   widens it.

## If you're in crisis (not just financial)

- **US/Canada:** 988 (call or text)
- **UK:** Samaritans, 116 123
- **International:** [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com/)
- **Crisis Text Line:** text HOME to 741741 (US/Canada/UK/Ireland)

You don't have to be "bad enough" to call. Overwhelmed counts.
